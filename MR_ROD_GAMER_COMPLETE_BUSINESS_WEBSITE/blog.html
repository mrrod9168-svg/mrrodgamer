GAMING

How to build a better gaming setup on a budget

A strong setup starts with the experience you want, not the most expensive equipment. Prioritize a comfortable control setup, stable connectivity, cooling, audio and a display that matches your games.

For mobile gaming, a good phone stand, reliable controller where supported, earphones and a cool environment can make a major difference. For PC or console gaming, spend first on the components that directly affect your gameplay and upgrade gradually.

SECURITY

5 practical ways to improve your digital security

  1. Use unique, strong passwords and a password manager.
  2. Turn on multi-factor authentication for important accounts.
  3. Keep your phone, computer and apps updated.
  4. Be cautious with unexpected links, files and login requests.
  5. Back up important data and review account recovery options.

Cybersecurity is strongest when good habits are combined with appropriate technical controls.

ENERGY

What to consider before installing a solar system

Start with your appliances and usage. List the loads you want to run, estimate how long they operate, and decide which loads are essential during outages. A proper site assessment can then guide the inverter, battery and panel configuration.

Installation quality matters too: correct cable sizing, protection, grounding, battery placement and system configuration are important for a safe and reliable installation.

CCTV

Planning a CCTV system for your property

Think about entrances, gates, blind spots, lighting, storage and who needs remote access. Camera selection should match the environment and the identification detail you need. Professional installation helps ensure coverage, cable protection and correct configuration.
